External Account Deletion Request Portal

Delete Your OrderKart Account

Portal Type: External Account Deletion Request Turnaround: 24–48 Hours Last Updated: 31 August 2026
Important Notice: This web page serves as the official External Account Deletion Request Portal. Deletion requests submitted remotely via email are manually verified against registered store records and processed within 24 to 48 hours. For instant automated deletion, please use the in-app deletion button inside the OrderKart mobile application.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the OrderKart mobile application installed on your Android smartphone, you can delete your account directly inside the app in seconds:

1
Open the OrderKart app on your device.
2
Tap on the My Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Scroll down to the Information & Support section.
4
Tap Delete Account (highlighted in red).
5
Review the on-screen confirmation details regarding cloud credential purge and store ledger preservation.
6
Tap Yes, Delete Account. Your credentials are permanently removed from the cloud database, local cache is wiped, and you are returned to the login screen.

Method 2: Remote Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application, lost access to your smartphone, or prefer to request deletion remotely, you can submit an account deletion request via email or WhatsApp.

⚠️ Can't access the app?

Send an account deletion request to our compliance team at supportorderkart@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 90211 07009.

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Full Name (as registered on your OrderKart account)
  • Registered 10-digit Mobile Phone Number
  • Customer Member Code (e.g. MEM101, if known)
🔒 Security Notice: NEVER send your account password or PIN by email.
✉️ Send Deletion Request Email

Transparency: What Data is Deleted vs Retained

Data That is Permanently Purged:
  • Cloud authentication credentials, password hashes, and security PINs.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push notification tokens.
  • Active authentication session tokens and cached local customer profile data.

Data That May Be Retained & Statutory Basis:

Under applicable Indian laws and regulatory standards, certain commercial records cannot be immediately erased upon account deletion:

  • Taxation & Accounting Records: In compliance with the Indian Income Tax Act 1961 and Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act 2017, completed sales receipts, invoices, and physical merchant ledger balances are retained in the shop's local point-of-sale system for statutory audit periods.
  • Fraud Prevention & Dispute Resolution: Essential transaction histories may be retained to resolve pending financial disputes or prevent repeat fraudulent order placement.

Processing Timeline

In-app account deletions take effect immediately. Remote web and email deletion requests are verified and processed within 24 to 48 hours of receipt.
